The Problem

Agents that execute a step. Not a plan.

Stops after the bridge

Chat-first agents complete a swap or a bridge, then stop cold at the next specific step — depositing into a particular protocol — and hand the task back to you.

No memory between sessions

Every request starts from zero. Your gas reserve, your preferred protocol, your risk tolerance — none of it carries over, so you restate it every time.

Never watches, only reacts

Ask once, it runs once. Nothing monitors your positions in the background or acts on your behalf while you're not there.

How It Works

One goal in, fully executed.

01 · STATE

State Reading

Real on-chain reads across every chain the wallet holds assets on — no mocked balances.

02 · GOAL

Goal Parsing

An LLM turns free text plus current wallet state into a structured goal.

03 · PLAN

Planning

A dependency graph, informed by a live on-chain yield lookup, then deterministically validated.

04 · CONFIRM

Confirmation

The plan is shown before anything moves — a routed conversation, not a blind yes/no gate.

05 · EXECUTE

Execution

Every step simulated before signing, capped at a hard limit, driven by real measured output.

What You Get

From one-time goals to always-on execution.

One confirmation, full execution

Waypoint coordinates transfers, swaps, bridges, and Aave supply, borrow, withdraw, and repay steps, then reports every confirmed transaction hash.

Automations created from chat

Describe a recurring or conditional goal naturally. Waypoint creates the automation card, runs DCA schedules, and monitors Aave health factor for pre-authorized repayment.

Bring any execution wallet

Create a wallet or import one you already control. Waypoint reads token metadata from chain, keeps wallets isolated, and shows live balances and DeFi positions.
